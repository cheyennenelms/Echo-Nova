# Echo Nova — Amplify the Data. Feel the Sound.

A music data analytics brand concept built for independent artists and music creators who need to understand — and grow — their audience.

## The idea

Most artists have access to streaming analytics but can't use them effectively. Echo Nova bridges that gap: a brand and platform concept that transforms complex streaming data into visual, story-driven insights artists can actually act on — connecting data with emotion, not just numbers.

## What I built

- **Brand identity end-to-end:** name, logo system (soundwave + nova starburst), neon-gradient visual identity, tagline, and brand mantra (Insight, Emotion, Impact)
- **Brand positioning:** positioned against traditional analytics platforms — artist-focused, visual-first, built for creators rather than enterprises
- **Product concept:** an artist analytics platform with custom dashboards translating streaming data into clear, actionable insights for release and promotion decisions
- **Partnership strategy:** scoped Spotify (streaming data, audience insights) and TikTok (trend and discovery data) integrations
- **Go-to-market:** freemium pricing with tiered subscriptions, channel strategy, and integrated marketing across TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube
